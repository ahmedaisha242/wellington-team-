The Pyramid

Understanding Fragrance Notes

Every great perfume is composed in three layers, revealing itself over time.

▲

Top Notes

The first impression — light, volatile citrus and herbal scents that last 15–30 minutes.

◆

Heart Notes

The core character — floral and spicy elements that emerge after 20 minutes and linger for hours.

▼

Base Notes

The lasting foundation — deep woods, resins and musks that can stay on skin for a full day.
